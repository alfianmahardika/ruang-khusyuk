Original annotations: Unicode code-point offsets into the Tanzil.net Uthmani text (copy of ca. April 2017).
Changes by Best Muslim: offsets re-mapped (character diff) onto the bundled Tanzil Uthmani 1.1 text; rule names
encoded as one-letter codes and offsets delta-encoded for size (60,023 of 60,057 annotations mapped exactly;
34 dropped where the underlying characters changed between Tanzil versions). Script: tools/tajweed_align.py.
Codes: w hamzat al-wasl, l lam shamsiyyah, s silent, a madd 2, b madd 2/4/6, c madd muttasil, d madd munfasil,
e madd lazim 6, g ghunnah, f ikhfa, h ikhfa shafawi, i idgham with ghunnah, n idgham without ghunnah,
o idgham shafawi, j idgham mutajanisayn, p idgham mutaqaribayn, q iqlab, k qalqalah.
