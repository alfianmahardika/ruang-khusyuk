text above, unchanged (split per surah; JSON field "ar"), plus:

  needs permission from the translator/publisher (Kemenag / LPMQ).

  public domain, text as distributed by Tanzil (en.pickthall).
- meta.json: surah/juz/hizb/page/sajdah metadata from Tanzil
  quran-data.xml; Indonesian surah names/meanings written by Kusyu.
